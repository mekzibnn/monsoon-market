Monsoon Market — a round-based three.js tactical FPS. OTTER attacks, HERON
defends two named sites (Spice Row and Fish Pier) in a rain-slick night
market on stilts at dusk: money between rounds, a buy menu with six weapons
plus gear and three grenade types, a charge to arm and defuse, and bots that
route, hold, listen and shoot back. First to 7 rounds takes the market.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click to play: WASD to
move, mouse to look, click to fire, R to reload, Ctrl to crouch, B for the
buy menu, hold E to arm or defuse the charge.
